## A. Cost and hit-time model

Use D for charged 24-hour node-days; a 12-hour lane-rep costs 0.5 D. At 178 arms and r=2.2–3.4 live cells/arm-rep, the baseline rate is 356r=783.2–1210.4 live cell fronts/node-day.

Let N=35,925 retained cell orbits, alpha=N_live/N, and eta the throughput factor for startup, replay, and idle arms at range boundaries relative to the reported baseline. Retained cells are not necessarily live; `cells_done` includes profile-dead cells. The full one-direction baseline sweep cost is

    S0 = alpha*N / (356*r*eta)
       = (29.7–45.9) * alpha/eta node-days.

If all retained cells are live and eta=1, this is 59–92 twelve-hour reps for one direction, or 119–183 reps for both. The handoff's estimate of about 30 reps for both directions implicitly needs only about 16–25% of retained cells live, or a different throughput assumption. Count distinct live cells to resolve this; do not mix raw windows, retained cells, and cells yielding a full candidate front.

The range table has 222 forward plus 222 reversed ownership units: 444 units. The 456 initial submissions include 12 extra workhorse repetitions on Fir. Ownership units are not completion-time estimates; a unit can require multiple repetitions.

For M=500k and per-live-cell cost C(K,B), a useful first approximation is

    C(K,B) = B*t_stream(M) + sum over buffers of K*t_complete(buffer,K).

Use min(K, actual buffer population) for short cells. Let g be the measured streaming share at K0=50k,B0=1, and initially assume equal mean completion cost across ranks/buffers:

    C(K,B)/C0 ≈ B*[g + (1-g)*K/50,000].

Completion-dominated full-sweep costs, before alpha/eta scaling:

| K | Buffers B | Cost multiple | One-direction node-days |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 12,500 | 1 | 0.25 | 7.4–11.5 |
| 50,000 | 1 | 1 | 29.7–45.9 |
| 175,000 | 1 | 3.5 | 103.9–160.5 |
| 175,000 | 2 | 7 | 207.8–321.1 |

With nonzero streaming cost these multiples become 0.25+0.75g, 1, 3.5−2.5g, and 7−5g. Higher-rank candidates can have different completion costs, so measure rather than extrapolate that part. For a marginal G2 pass following G, 300k of its nominal 350k completions are new if exact duplicate work is skipped. Re-streaming both buffers still costs time.

Define capture R(K,B,b) as the probability that a solution-bearing representative lies in one of buffers 1..B, at sorted rank <=K, and is reached by completer budget b, under the actual canonicalization and direction. Let Lambda(K,B,b) be the expected number of detectable solution-bearing cells in the whole live-cell sweep. Under a Poisson/uniform-location planning model,

    P(hit by D) = 1-exp[-Lambda*min(D/S,1)].

Before the sweep ends, the hazard is Lambda/S. A median occurs at D50=S*log(2)/Lambda only if Lambda>=log(2); otherwise the pass finishes before achieving 50% hit probability. At full sweep, Lambda=0.1,1,3 gives 9.5%,63.2%,95.0% success respectively. Conditional on the pass containing at least one detectable cell,

    E[D_hit | hit in pass] = S*[1/Lambda - 1/(exp(Lambda)-1)].

These are conditional model calculations, not estimates of Lambda. For exactly one uniformly located detectable target, the conditional mean is S/2. Without evidence that a finite sequence of passes contains a hit, there is no finite unconditional expected time justified by these data.

The three selected known successes in buffers 1–2 do not identify R or Lambda at n44, or the sorted-rank and cap parts of R. They were also collected under different historical canonicalization policies. Do not substitute R(50k,1)=2/3 or R(175k,2)=1. Compare marginal capture/cost, delta R/delta C, once exact retained ranks and new production timings are measured. F and FR need their intersection accounted for; different enumeration directions are not independent trials.

Confidence: high in arithmetic and conditional model; unidentified hit-rate parameters. Re-measure r after the pin fix. The earlier 25%/50% retained-orbit estimates are symmetry heuristics, not measured fractions.

## B. Joint reachability: insertion and production measurement

No saved real n44 residual-row trace was found in the repository. Therefore **no measured production rejection percentage is available**. The earlier 30.6% toy aggregate-space rejection is not a valid estimate after 2.11a/b, 2.12, the current NPAF bounds, and parent profile filtering. A >=20% CPU reduction remains a plausible experiment target, not a forecast.

In `fh_abp_filter(d)` (solver line 760), both positions of quad d have already been placed. Insert the new row test **after the existing componentwise capacity loop and before `dst.push_back(idx)`**:

```cpp
if (ok && !fh_abp_joint_ok(row, d + 1)) ok = false;
if (ok) dst.push_back(idx);
```

The helper's depth argument is the first unplaced mirror-pair index. Build residuals from `row.k[c]-FH_PA[c]` and `row.r[c]-FH_PB[c]`. Precompute remaining component counts by depth once. At this call the exceptional AB root has already been consumed, including when d=0; do not subtract it a second time.

For n44/mod6 the principal coupled components are {0,2} and {3,5}. Their numbers of remaining ordinary quads are respectively `FH_ABP_TIC[0]-FH_PLACED[0]` and `FH_ABP_TIC[3]-FH_PLACED[3]`. Each equals the remaining position count in the other class of its pair. For each, use residual order (A_r,B_r,A_s,B_s):

```cpp
static inline bool reach4(int x, int y, int z, int w, int q) {
  int h0=x+y+z+w, h1=x+y-z-w;
  int h2=x-y+z-w, h3=x-y-z+w;
  if (h0%4 || h1%4 || h2%4 || h3%4) return false;
  int hnorm=abs(h0)+abs(h1)+abs(h2)+abs(h3);
  return hnorm <= 4*q && (4*q-hnorm)%8 == 0;
}
```

This is the exact lattice, L1-capacity and parity test expressed without division. Existing invariants may make some congruence tests redundant; retain them in the first validated implementation rather than assuming new rejection from them.

Handle self-reflected class {1} using even residuals, residual-A+residual-B divisible by 4, and each magnitude <=2q. Class {4} still contains the unplaced central column: existentially subtract one of its four sign pairs before applying that self-component test. Do not apply `reach4` to two copies of a self-reflected class, or treat the center as an ordinary quad. The normal leaf check remains unchanged. General n/modulus support needs component descriptors rather than hardcoded n44 indices.

For production measurement, shadow-test a deterministic sample of calls across all classes and early/middle/late depths, after the old capacity filter. Record per depth: rows surviving the old filter, rows rejected additionally, calls made empty by the new test, old-empty calls, profile-list sizes, and time spent. Include both direction fronts and budget-aborted candidates. Keep the old survivor lists unchanged during shadow collection. Then run a paired old/new benchmark on the same saved candidate and cell fixtures: row rejection alone does not price the subtrees removed.

If f is the old completion fraction of total time, s the fraction of old completion work removed, and h the added test overhead relative to old completion time, the total CPU reduction is f*(s-h). For example, f=0.9 and h=0.05 require s>=0.2722 to save 20% overall. Removing 30% of rows while leaving at least one row at every node may save almost nothing. Rejecting the last row early can save a great deal. This is why a numerical production forecast needs the trace/paired test.

Start with static profile-row prefiltering and the two coupled residual components; measure the full exact helper separately. Pre-register all-reference retention, exact small-n comparisons, and >=20% paired CPU reduction on held-out production fixtures before fleet adoption. Confidence: high in the condition and insertion point, low in any claimed production speedup without measurement.

## D. Equivalence under the extra transformations

In general, yes: distinct orbits under the 1,024 subgroup can merge under global alternation or the C,D quad transformation 4↔5. The full abstract group has order 4,096; individual solution orbits can be smaller because of stabilizers. See [Đoković §3–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1002.1414).

For the three compared campaign/WZ pairs, this cannot happen. Define L=sum_s |N_C(s)+N_D(s)|. Negation, reversal and C/D swapping preserve the combined correlations; the quad transformation also preserves them; global alternation multiplies shift s by (-1)^s, leaving L unchanged. Recomputed values are:

| n | Campaign | WZ |
|---|---:|---:|
| 41 | 124 | 140 |
| 42 | 150 | 142 |
| 43 | 130 | 134 |

All six files pass direct NPAF checks. Different L proves inequivalence under the full group. Equal L would be inconclusive; future pairs then require full group closure/canonicalization, without restricting the intermediate signatures since alternation can change them. Confidence: proved for the three supplied comparisons; not a comparison against every solution ever published.
